Sink unclogging state cards

One animated card per state, built from icons and 1 to 3 words, following the team's Ray-Ban Display design guide. Every card is shown on the same bathroom view. Each also exists as a transparent PNG to layer over your own footage.

Animated master: design/state-cards/svg/ holds one self-contained animated SVG per card, moving only position, rotation, scale and opacity, as Meta's web-app motion rules ask. It drops in as is on the web-app route and is the source for MP4 (toolkit route) and PNG-sequence exports. With reduced motion turned on, each card shows its still pose.

Stills: PNGs are 1200 × 1200 (2×) with a transparent background, in design/state-cards/png/, showing each card's resting pose. To imitate the glasses, put them on a layer set to Screen blend mode over slightly darkened footage, right of centre. The sound and voice line under each card is the audio cue to play with it.
