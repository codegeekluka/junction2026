**Headline numbers:** 2,000 builders · 48 h of building · €100K in prizes · 100+ countries · Hype Areena, Espoo · 13–15.11.2026. Theme: "Someone will move the center of gravity. The mover could be you."

**Judging: how the winner is chosen** (built and judged on the Junction Platform)
1. **Partner tracks:** each Challenge Partner chooses their own track winners against their own criteria.
2. **Top 5 peer review:** the Main Event winner is decided first through face-to-face peer review to select the top 5 projects.
3. **Live pitch & grand jury:** the top 5 pitch in front of a live audience, and a grand jury chooses the overall winner.

## 3. Hardware desk (glasses, kits, 3D printing)
Source: https://2026.hackjunction.com/hardware. It is live and changes constantly; this snapshot is from 23:29 Fri.

**How it works**
1. **Choose your hardware:** browse availability, then sign in with your Junction account and choose your first and second choices.
2. **Send a team request:** one request per team. A request does not reserve stock; a volunteer confirms your equipment at the desk.
3. **Collect, build, return:** visit the desk with your team name and photo ID, and return the equipment to a volunteer when you're done.

**Borrowing rules & return instructions**
- Borrowing: **Leave a photo ID at the desk as a deposit.**
- Returning: Bring items back to the hardware desk and wait for a volunteer to confirm the return.
- ★ Special item: one per team at a time.
